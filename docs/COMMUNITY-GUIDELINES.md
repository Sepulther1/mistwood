# Community Guidelines (a.k.a. Please Don’t Be “That Person”)

Welcome to **Mistwood**. We’re here to have fun, build weirdly good things, and not set anything on fire (metaphorically). A few house rules:

## The Five-Point Spell of Being Decent
1. **Be kind.** Wit is welcome; cruelty isn’t.
2. **No harassment.** No stalking, doxxing, or targeted pile-ons.
3. **No hate.** Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, and other bigotry are a hard nope.
4. **No cheating or exploits.** Report bugs, don’t farm them.
5. **No spam or scams.** Your crypto pyramid goes elsewhere.

## Reporting (aka “Tell the grown-ups”)
- **In-game:** `@report <what happened>`
- **Discord:** DM ModMail (link in #welcome)
- **Email:** support@yourdomain.tld

Include: who/what/when/where + proof (screens, logs). We’re quick, not psychic.

## Consequences (we really prefer not to use these)
| Step       | What it means                          | Typical duration |
|------------|----------------------------------------|------------------|
| Warning    | “Knock it off.”                        | –                |
| Mute       | Read-only; cool-down.                  | hours–days       |
| Temp Ban   | Take a vacation from us.               | days–weeks       |
| Permaban   | All roads lead away from Mistwood.     | forever          |

Escalation depends on severity and history. Appeal once via ModMail; we’ll review.

## Name & Content Rules (PG-13-ish)
We draw the line at slurs, sexual content involving minors, and shock gore. Keep usernames & bios chill.

## Final Boss Clause
Staff may act outside the letter of these rules to protect the community’s safety and vibe. We’ll document in **OPERATIONS.md** (moderation runbook) and keep you posted.

AI-Assisted Contributions (encouraged, responsibly)

You may use AI to draft code/docs. You are responsible for:

Licensing compliance (no copying GPL-only code, etc.)

Explaining design choices in the PR description

Adding/adjusting tests and verifying runtime behavior

Add a footer: AI-assist: yes and list tools used.

Preview Environments (per-contributor server)

We plan to run a GitHub Actions job that boots a temporary Mistwood instance for your PR and posts its URL in the PR (ephemeral, auto-teardown).

Until that lands, run locally and attach logs/screenshots; we’ll replicate if needed.
