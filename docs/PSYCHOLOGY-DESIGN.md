# Psychology & Sociology in Mistwood

## Player Traits
- Big Five-ish axes mapped to mechanics (risk, cooperation, novelty).
- Long-term “virtues” that unlock social contracts and civic roles.

## Motivators
- Intrinsic: mastery, autonomy, purpose (civic impact).
- Extrinsic: titles, land deeds, dividends, festival trophies.

## Social Pressure & Norms
- Faction reputation, guild cohesion, city pride.
- Public works boards; visible “contributors of the week”.

## Anti-toxicity Loops
- Fast feedback on good acts (micro-rewards).
- Cool-downs and costs on griefing; restorative quests after penalties.
